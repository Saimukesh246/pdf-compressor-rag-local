ℹ️ System Architecture & Engine Pipeline

          Offline-First AI
        

        

          
This platform executes a 6-stage hybrid compression & semantic pipeline:

          

            

              01
              
PyMuPDF & OCR Inspector

              
Extracts font density, stream object counts, and executes Tesseract OCR fallback for scanned documents.

            

            

              02
              
Deterministic Rule Engine

              
Evaluates fast-path heuristics for clear text-heavy or scanned document categories.

            

            

              03
              
FAISS Vector Strategy Decider

              
Executes semantic similarity search against knowledge base vectors for ambiguous documents.

            

            

              04
              
Security, Watermarking & Redaction

              
AES-256 encryption, diagonal vector watermarking, and regex-based automated PII masking.

            

            

              05
              
Ghostscript Safe Rebuild

              
Deflates streams (`garbage=4`) and downsamples images without losing transparency or vector crispness.

            

            

              06
              
LLM RAG & Converter Suite

              
PDF to Images PNG ZIP conversion, Markdown export, page-level windowed chunking for Q&A, and Mermaid mindmaps.
