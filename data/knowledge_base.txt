Image-heavy PDF:
Downscale images to 150 DPI
Convert images to JPEG format
Apply lossy image compression and downsampling

Text-heavy PDF:
Remove unused fonts and subsets
Optimize PDF object streams
Enable deflate stream compression

Scanned PDF:
Apply deskew and grayscale downsampling
Compress background images aggressively
Maintain OCR text layer if present

Mixed Content PDF:
Balance image resolution at 200 DPI
Clean unused objects and deduplicate font streams
Apply adaptive stream deflate compression
